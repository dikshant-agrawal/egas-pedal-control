eGAS Motor Control · Digital Twin

Interactive twin of the eGAS software component (swc_egas.c) — a joystick-driven electronic-throttle service running a closed-loop PID speed controller. The firmware's control law runs here in the browser against a simulated motor plant, so you can retune the gains and watch the response before touching hardware.
