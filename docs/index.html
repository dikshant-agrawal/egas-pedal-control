eGAS Digital Twin

An interactive twin of the swc_egas software component — a joystick-driven electronic-throttle service with a closed-loop PID speed controller. The firmware's control law runs here in the browser against a simulated motor plant — or switch to Hardware mode to plot the live serial stream from the board.
